Before you write

Answers to the most common requests

Emails are not being sent to my customers

Check three things first: automation must be explicitly activated, merchant test mode must be off, and the subscription must be active. If all three are fine, look at the dispute in the dashboard — it may have been placed under manual review because the shipment data was unclear.

A dispute is stuck in manual review

That is intentional whenever the shipment state is unknown, the fulfilment data is mixed or partial, the dispute reason is unsupported, or customer information is missing. Handle the case yourself in Shopify; the app will not send an automatic email for it.

I want to change what an email says

Open the templates screen and edit the template for that dispute reason and shipment state. You can preview it, send a test to your own address, and restore the default at any time.

How do I cancel?

Uninstall the app or cancel the subscription from your Shopify admin. Uninstalling immediately clears access tokens and disables automation, and your store's data is removed after Shopify's shop redaction process runs.

Can you send emails for disputes from another payment gateway?

Not in this version. Dispute Guard covers Shopify Payments disputes only. If a different gateway matters to you, tell us — it helps us prioritise.

Still stuck? Write to disputeguardapp@gmail.com with your store domain and, if relevant, the dispute reference.
