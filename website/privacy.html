1. Who we are

Dispute Guard (“Dispute Guard”, “we”, “us”) provides an embedded Shopify application that helps merchants manage customer communication around Shopify Payments disputes. This policy explains how we handle personal data in connection with that application and with this website.

You can reach us at any time at disputeguardapp@gmail.com.

2. Our role and yours

When you install Dispute Guard on your Shopify store, you remain the controller of your customers' personal data. We act as a processor on your behalf: we read order, customer and dispute data from Shopify only to perform the functions you have configured, and we act on your instructions and on the settings you choose inside the app.

For merchant account information — the store identity, your settings, your billing status and your correspondence with our support team — we act as a controller.

3. What data we collect

Merchant and store data

  • Your Shopify store domain, store name and basic shop identity returned by Shopify
  • An encrypted Shopify access token bundle used to make API calls on your behalf
  • Your app settings: support contact details, reply-to address, automation and test-mode state
  • Your email templates and any edits you make to them
  • Subscription and plan status as reported by Shopify's billing systems
  • Support correspondence you send to us by email

Dispute and order data

  • Shopify Payments dispute identifiers, reasons, statuses, amounts and currencies
  • The related order reference and its fulfilment records
  • Tracking numbers, carrier statuses and delivery events used to classify shipment state
  • Refund records and their transaction statuses, shown for context

Customer personal data

  • The customer's name and email address as held on the Shopify order
  • A keyed hash of the customer email, used to detect whether the recipient has changed
  • The rendered content of the email that was prepared or sent, including its subject line

We do not receive or store card numbers, bank details or payment credentials. Those never leave Shopify and its payment providers.

Operational data

  • Webhook delivery identifiers and minimal event markers, used to prevent duplicate processing
  • Email logs recording what was sent, when, and with what outcome
  • Application and error logs needed to keep the service running correctly

5. Shopify permissions

Dispute Guard requests read-only Shopify access scopes:

  • read_orders — to match a dispute to its order and read fulfilment and tracking data
  • read_customers — to obtain the customer name and email address for the message
  • read_shopify_payments_disputes — to see disputes raised against your store

Customer name and email fall under Shopify's protected customer data rules and require Shopify's approval. We request the minimum needed to send the email you have configured. The app holds no write permissions, so it cannot create, modify or delete anything in your store.

6. Emails we send

Customer emails are transactional messages about an existing dispute on an existing order. They are not marketing, and Dispute Guard does not send marketing to your customers.

  • The recipient is always taken from the live Shopify order at send time. If the address has changed since processing, the dispute is flagged for your review instead of being sent to a different person.
  • Messages are sent from our authenticated application sending domain, with your support address as the reply-to, so customer replies reach you rather than us.
  • A dispute can generate at most one initial automatic email. We do not run follow-up sequences.
  • Template content is restricted to a safe set of substitutions and basic formatting; no scripts or executable content can be inserted into a message.

7. Storage and retention

Data is stored in a database used solely for the application, with each store's records isolated from every other store's. We keep data only as long as it is needed:

Failed background job records24 hours
Store data after the app is uninstalledAccess tokens cleared immediately; remaining tenant data removed after Shopify's shop redaction, and inactive stores are purged automatically
Dispute and email log metadata used for your dashboardFor the life of your installation, subject to the expiries above
Support correspondenceAs long as needed to handle the request and keep a record of it

Deleting records in our database does not erase copies held by external services outside our control, such as an email provider's own delivery logs or backups, which follow those providers' retention schedules.

8. Security measures

  • All traffic to the application uses HTTPS.
  • Shopify access tokens are stored encrypted and are never exposed to browser JavaScript.
  • Every embedded request is verified against Shopify's session token; a browser-supplied store identifier can never determine which store's data is returned.
  • Incoming webhooks are verified with an HMAC signature over the untouched request body, and duplicate deliveries are rejected.
  • Stored sensitive content, previews and privacy exports are encrypted at rest.
  • Each store's records are queried through tenant-scoped policies so one merchant cannot reach another's data.
  • Sensitive values are kept out of application logs, and request-body logging is not enabled in production.

No system can be guaranteed completely secure, but we design conservatively and keep the amount of personal data we hold to the minimum the service needs.

10. Privacy requests and deletion

Dispute Guard implements Shopify's mandatory privacy webhooks. When Shopify sends one of these requests, we act on it as follows:

  • Customer data request — we prepare an encrypted export of the data we hold about that customer for your store, and make it available to you in the app under Settings. You verify the requester and deliver the export through a secure channel, then mark the request completed. We do not email exports automatically.
  • Customer redaction — we clear the customer's stored details for the matched orders: tracking data, order references, recipient details and stored email previews. Pending deliveries are cancelled, affected disputes are marked redacted so the data is not fetched again, and any existing export becomes invalid.
  • Shop redaction — after a store uninstalls, we delete that store's data from the application.

Uninstalling the app immediately clears our stored access tokens and disables all automation for that store.

A customer can also contact you, the merchant, directly; you can raise the request with us at disputeguardapp@gmail.com with “Privacy request” in the subject line, and we will help you fulfil it within the applicable deadline.

Please note that once an email has been accepted by our delivery provider it cannot be recalled, and the customer's own mailbox is outside our control.

11. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your personal data, to receive a copy of it in a portable form, and to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

If you are a customer of a store that uses Dispute Guard, please contact that store first — they are the controller of your data and can raise the request with us. If you are a merchant, write to us directly and we will respond within the period required by applicable law.

12. This website

This website is informational. It does not set advertising or tracking cookies, and it does not run third-party analytics or advertising scripts. The contact form on this site does not send anything to a server of ours — it opens a message in your own email application, addressed to disputeguardapp@gmail.com, which you choose whether to send.

The site loads a web font from Google Fonts, which means your browser makes a request to Google's servers when a page loads. Standard server logs kept by our hosting provider may record IP addresses and request details for security and reliability purposes.

13. Children

Dispute Guard is a business tool intended for merchants. It is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under the age of 16 other than as it may appear in a merchant's own order records.

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the application changes or as legal requirements evolve. The “last updated” date at the top of this page always reflects the current version. Where a change materially affects how we handle personal data, we will make reasonable efforts to notify merchants using the app.
